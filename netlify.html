HOW TO PUBLISH YOUR WEBSITE USING NETLIFY

Step 1:

Go to Netlify.com website to sign up


Step 2:

Once you sign up, answers the questions and press Continue to deploy


Step 3:

Select "Try Netlify Drop, then you can drag the folder that your files are in, or click the rectangle to select the folder


Step 4:

Go to Site, then upload your folder again


Step 5:

On the top left corner, go to Site Configuration, you can change your website name in here


Step 6:

Go to Site Overview and click the link to your website. Now your website is published to the world!
